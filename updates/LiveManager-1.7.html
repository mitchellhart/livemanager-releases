LiveManager 1.7

Ideas that start on your phone now have somewhere to go.

  • Capture ideas on your phone. Share a voice memo or a video of yourself playing into LiveManager on iPhone, or tap + to pick a video from Photos. It becomes a real song — playable, ratable, filed into an album — and syncs here with no Ableton project behind it.
  • Watch the original. A capture from a video keeps a link back to the clip in your photo library, so you can go and see what your hands were doing.
  • Turn an idea into a project. Start the project in Live as you normally would, then select the idea and the new song and choose Merge Into. The recording is filed into the project folder, where Live picks it up under Places → Current Project.
  • Captures play from the Mac player like any other demo, with their waveform and next/back stepping.
  • Renaming a song now works on the iPhone too — tap its name.
