LiveManager 1.5

Switching looping off now sticks.

Fixed

  • Looping stays off. Turning the loop off and moving to another demo used to bring the brackets straight back, and any demo you'd ever pressed ⌘L on would re-loop itself every time it played. Off now means off — on that demo, on the next one, and after a relaunch.
  • The loop button still remembers where you left the brackets, so switching looping back on picks up the section you were working on rather than starting from the ends of the demo.

One-time note: the first time you open 1.5, every demo starts unlooped. Your bracket positions are all still saved — one press of ⌘L on a demo brings its section back.

Also since 1.3

  • Logic Pro support. Logic projects appear as songs alongside your Ableton sets, with tempo, time signature, track count and alternatives listed as versions.
  • Volume slider in the player bar, remembered between launches.
  • A repeated project file could crash the app on launch; LiveManager now repairs the duplicate instead of stopping.
