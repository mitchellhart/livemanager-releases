LiveManager 1.2

This version adds automatic updates — from here on, LiveManager can update itself in one click instead of you re-downloading it.

New

  • Automatic updates. LiveManager checks for new versions in the background, or on demand from LiveManager → Check for Updates…
  • Waveforms. The player bar draws the demo's waveform, so you can see the arrangement while you listen.
  • Loop a section. Hit the loop button (⌘L) and drag the brackets to cycle any part of a demo. Loop points are remembered per song and sync to the iPhone app.
  • iPhone companion. Listen to your library and set loop points on the go — see Help → Get the iPhone App…

Improved

  • A warning now appears if iCloud sync isn't working, instead of failing silently.
  • Song details are aligned and easier to scan; notes have room to breathe and commit when you click away.
