LiveManager 1.9

Identify guitar notes and chords, and share demos from your Mac.

  • Experimental Chord Detector. Open Tools → Chord Detector to listen to your guitar through the microphone, check individual notes and tuning, and estimate common chords. Audio is analyzed locally and is never saved or uploaded.
  • Share demos. Share an audio demo from the song details using the macOS share sheet. LiveManager places a copy in the LiveManager Shares folder in iCloud Drive.
  • Duplicate synced entries now appear only once in song lists and sidebar counts.
